# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

> "Part of the journey is the end." — Tony Stark

## Overview
**Avengers: Endgame** is a *2019 superhero film* produced by Marvel Studios. Set five years after Thanos wiped out half of all life in the universe, the surviving Avengers assemble one final time to pull off a "Time Heist," reverse the Blip, and restore order to the universe.

---

## Main Characters
* **Tony Stark / Iron Man** (`Robert Downey Jr.`) – The genius billionaire who cracks time travel and makes the ultimate sacrifice.
* **Steve Rogers / Captain America** (`Chris Evans`) – The steadfast leader of the Avengers who proves worthy of Mjolnir.
* **Thor** (`Chris Hemsworth`) – The God of Thunder dealing with the grief of his past failure.
* **Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow** (`Scarlett Johansson`) – The strategist keeping the team together who gives her life for the Soul Stone.
* **Bruce Banner / Smart Hulk** (`Mark Ruffalo`) – The combined brains and brawn who performs the snap to bring everyone back.

## Top 3 Best Moments
1. **The Portals Scene:** Every fallen hero returning to the battlefield as Captain America finally says, *"Avengers, assemble."*
2. **Captain America Wielding Mjolnir:** The payoff to a setup that started all the way back in *Age of Ultron*.
3. **"I Am Iron Man":** Tony Stark's final line before snapping Thanos and his army away.
